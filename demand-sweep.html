QAWP against fixed-time control from free flow to beyond capacity

Delay, fairness, throughput and switching of QAWP and both fixed-time baselines for 1000–4000 veh/h.

Mean of three simulation runs per point; 50 % CAVs, 300 pedestrians/h, minimum green 10 s, w₁ = w₂ = 0.5. The dashed line marks the capacity of about 3500 veh/h.

The design programme changes phase 160 times per hour by construction (four green phases in a 90 s cycle). Above capacity, delay values are biased low because vehicles that never enter the network add no delay.
