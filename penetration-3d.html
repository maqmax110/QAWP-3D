Queue-aware signal control under mixed traffic: interactive results

Companion to the summary figure of the master’s thesis “Queue-Aware Delay Balancing at Automated Intersections under Mixed Traffic”, Technical University of Munich, 2026.

Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, hover for values. The camera icon at the top right of the chart saves a high-resolution image of the current view.

Average delay over CAV penetration (5–100 %, logarithmic axis) and vehicle demand (1000–3250 veh/h). Where the coloured surface lies below the grey one, QAWP with CAV perception outperforms the fixed-time programme. The blue surface shows QAWP with perfect information, the upper bound for perception-based control. All three controllers were simulated on identical traffic; each point is the mean of five paired simulation runs in SUMO, with equal queue and waiting-time weights (w₁ = w₂ = 0.5) and 300 pedestrians/h.

Average delay of QAWP with CAV perception over penetration and the queue weight w₁ (the waiting-time weight is w₂ = 1 − w₁), at 2500 veh/h and 300 pedestrians/h. Black points mark the best weight at each penetration; the grey plane is the fixed-time design programme. Each point is the mean of five simulation runs in SUMO.
