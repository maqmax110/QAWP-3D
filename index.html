Vehicle and pedestrian delay by CAV penetration and queue weight

Interactive companion to the summary figure of the master’s thesis “Queue-Aware Delay Balancing at Automated Intersections under Mixed Traffic”, Technical University of Munich, 2026.

Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, hover for values.

The coloured surface shows the average delay of QAWP when its traffic state comes only from what connected automated vehicles can perceive (V2X reports and on-board ray casting with occlusion). The grey plane is the fixed-time design programme on the same traffic. Black points mark the best queue weight at each penetration rate.

Demand 2500 veh/h, 300 pedestrians/h. Each point is the mean of five simulation runs in SUMO; the waiting-time weight is w₂ = 1 − w₁. Penetration rates are plotted to scale (5, 10, 20, 30, 50 and 80 %).
